Morning Rituals

A simple set of conscious habits to begin the day with intention. Right after waking: rinse the nasal passages, clean the tongue with a scraper, and drink a glass of warm water. Then five minutes of awakening pranayama, and at least ten minutes of meditation. A small morning ritual that changes everything.

Wake a little earlier than you need to — enough to move through this sequence without rushing. In the Vedic tradition this window before sunrise, Brahma Muhurta, is considered a time when the mind is naturally clear and receptive. Follow the breath practice above, then sit for the meditation, before food or phone.

2. Jala Neti — Nasal Cleansing
Watch on Dropbox
Morning Cleansing — covers Jala Neti, Tongue Scraping & Warm Water

Use a neti pot filled with warm, lightly salted water (about the salinity of tears). Standing over a sink, gently tilt your head to one side and pour the water through the upper nostril, allowing it to flow out of the other. Breathe through your mouth and move slowly, without force. Repeat on the other side, then softly dry the nasal passages.

3. Tongue Scraping
Watch on Dropbox
Morning Cleansing — covers Jala Neti, Tongue Scraping & Warm Water

Use a tongue scraper and clean from back to front 5-7 times with light pressure. Rinse the scraper after each pass. This supports oral freshness and helps reduce morning coating before hydration and breakfast.

4. Warm Water
Watch on Dropbox
Morning Cleansing — covers Jala Neti, Tongue Scraping & Warm Water

Sip a glass of warm (not hot) water upon waking. Drink slowly, allowing the body to receive it without rush. You may choose to sit quietly as you drink, noticing the warmth and the gradual sense of hydration.

5. Open the Room

Once your practice is done, open a window or the curtains and let natural light and air into the room. A small, physical signal that the day has begun — a way of inviting freshness into the space before you step out into it.
