Morning Rituals

Between your study days, the course never really stops. On the days you're not in class, three small practices are waiting for you — not more to carry, but something to hold you steady: a way to open the day with intention, move through it awake, and close it with meaning.

This is bhakti as everyday life, not theory — the quiet rituals that shape what it means to be a yogi off the mat. Nothing here is meant to feel like discipline. It's meant to feel like something you come back to, gladly, every morning.

Right after waking, before food or phone: rinse the nasal passages with warm salted water (Jala Neti), scrape the tongue clean from back to front, then sip a glass of warm water slowly. Three small acts that clear the body before the mind follows.
