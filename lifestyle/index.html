Bhakti Lifestyle Activities

Between your study days, the course never stops. On the days you're not in class, gentle daily practices are waiting for you — not to load you down, but to hold you: to open the day with intention, to move through it with awareness, and to close it with reflection.

This is bhakti in everyday life — not as theory, but as the very fabric of a yogi's life beyond the mat. These small rituals build a joyful discipline: not something you force yourself into, but something you keep returning to with pleasure.

Morning Rituals

A simple set of conscious habits to begin the day with intention. Right after waking: rinse the sinuses, scrape the tongue clean, and drink a glass of warm water. Then five minutes of awakening pranayama, and at least ten minutes of meditation. A small morning ritual that changes everything.

1

Morning Cleansing

Nasal rinse · tongue scraping · a glass of warm water

Right after you wake up, before anything else: gently rinse your sinuses, scrape the tongue clean, and drink a full glass of warm water. Three simple acts that clear the body before the mind has even fully woken up — the foundation everything else is built on.

Recipe of the Day

Every day we share a recipe that nourishes your yogic lifestyle from within. This is more than food: cooking with love and attention is one of the most accessible forms of bhakti — a daily act of devotion, care and presence. Let the kitchen become a place of meditation.

Journal

At the end of the day, take a few minutes to write down what you did, what you learned, what you noticed — even the smallest detail matters. Capture your key insights and moments of clarity. Over time, this practice becomes a living record of your transformation, and a powerful tool for self-observation, responsibility and growth.
