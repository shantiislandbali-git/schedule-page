Shanti Lifestyle

A calm morning structure drawn from traditional yogic practice — not required for your certificate, but the habit that actually changes things. Clearing the mind after waking matters as much as brushing your teeth; pranayama and meditation are the simplest tools for it. Begin with what fits your present rhythm, then let consistency do the quiet work.

Shatkriya

Traditional cleansing before practice — one short sequence, one video.

Morning Cleansing

5 min+30 pts

Scrape the tongue gently from back to front, five to seven times, rinsing after each pass. Follow with Jala Neti — a light saline rinse through each nostril — to clear the airway before pranayama. Finish with a full glass of warm water on an empty stomach.

Benefit: clears the body's surfaces before the breath work begins — the traditional preparation for pranayama.
